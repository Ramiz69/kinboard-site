Short description

Family screen for TV and iPad.

Turn the Apple TV or iPad you already own into the family screen: everyone's calendars, shared lists, kids' chores and the weather. Try everything free for 14 days.

Long description

Kinboard turns the Apple TV in your living room or the iPad on your kitchen wall into a family screen. Everyone's day, the grocery list, the kids' chores and the weather - readable from across the room, and always on.

No extra gadget to buy, no account to create, no subscription.

Everyone's calendar

  • Each person gets their own color, so you can tell at a glance who is where
  • Today and tomorrow side by side, with what's happening right now on top
  • Calendars from your iPhone and iPad, including Google and Outlook accounts added there
  • Any calendar you can subscribe to by link - school, sports club, holidays

Shared lists

  • Groceries, packing and to-dos that anyone can update from their iPhone
  • Tick items off right on the iPad
  • Open items appear on the screen and on the Apple TV Top Shelf

Kids' chores

  • Daily jobs for each child, on the days you choose
  • One to three stars per chore, and a weekly star count kids can see
  • Kids mark chores done themselves on the iPad

More on the screen

  • Weather for where you are
  • Countdowns to birthdays, trips and the next visit from Grandma
  • Family photos slowly changing behind the schedule
  • Night mode dims the screen at bedtime and wakes it in the morning

Made for a screen that stays on

  • Keeps the screen awake and refreshes itself
  • Gently shifts the layout to protect OLED screens
  • Big rounded type and high contrast, with Bold Text and Increase Contrast support

The whole family, one screen

Set everything up from your iPhone and invite your family. Everyone edits the same lists and calendars from their own iPhone, and changes appear on every screen within seconds.

Private by design

Kinboard has no servers of its own and no analytics. Everything syncs through your family's iCloud - the developer never sees your calendars, lists or photos.

Free trial, then one purchase

Everything is open for 14 days. After that, the clock, weather, countdowns, night mode and one calendar stay free. Kinboard+ is a one-time purchase that unlocks every calendar, shared lists, chores and stars, the photo background, family invitations and the Top Shelf - on Apple TV, iPad and iPhone, and for your whole family through Family Sharing.

Key features

  • Everyone's calendar, one color each. Today and tomorrow side by side, from the calendars on the family's iPhones and iPads (Google and Outlook included) and any calendar link.
  • Shared lists. Groceries, packing and to-dos anyone can update from their iPhone or tick off on the iPad; open items also appear on the Apple TV Top Shelf.
  • Kids' chores with stars. Daily jobs on the days you choose, one to three stars each, and a weekly star count the kids can see.
  • Weather, countdowns and photos. Local weather, countdowns to birthdays and trips, and family photos slowly changing behind the schedule.
  • Made to stay on. Keeps the screen awake, refreshes itself, dims at night, gently shifts the layout to protect OLED screens, and supports Bold Text and Increase Contrast.
  • Private by design. No account and no analytics. Everything syncs through the family's iCloud, and the developer never sees it.
