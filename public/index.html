Questions

Which devices do I need?

An Apple TV or an iPad for the family screen, and ideally an iPhone to set things up and edit them. You can also set up right on the iPad or the Apple TV, but the iPhone is the easiest. Kinboard requires iOS 26, iPadOS 26 or tvOS 26.

Do I need an account?

No. Kinboard syncs through iCloud, the same way your Notes and Reminders do. The developer never sees your calendars, lists or photos.

Is there a subscription?

No. Kinboard+ is a one-time purchase, with a 14-day free trial so you can see it on your own screen first. The clock, weather, countdowns, night mode and one calendar stay free after the trial.

Does it work with Google Calendar and Outlook?

Yes. Calendars from your iPhone or iPad appear on the screen, including Google and Outlook accounts added there, plus any calendar you can subscribe to by link (ICS or webcal), such as many school calendars. Kinboard only reads calendars and never changes them.

Can the whole family edit it?

Yes. On your iPhone, open Family → Invite Family Members. Everyone you invite sees and edits the same lists, calendars and countdowns from their own iPhone, and changes appear on every screen within seconds.

Does my family have to buy it too?

No. Kinboard+ works with Family Sharing: buy it once and it is unlocked for everyone in your Apple family group, on Apple TV, iPad and iPhone.

What happens when the trial ends?

The clock, weather, countdowns, night mode and one calendar keep working. Lists, chores, photos and the other calendars are kept and come back as soon as Kinboard+ is unlocked.
