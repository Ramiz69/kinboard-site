Questions

Which devices will it support?

Apple TV (tvOS) and iPad for the display, plus an iPhone companion for setup and editing. One purchase covers all of them.

Do I need an account?

No. Kinboard syncs through iCloud, the same way your Notes and Reminders do. We never see your calendar or photos.

Will there be a subscription?

No. Kinboard is a one-time purchase, with a 14-day free trial so you can see it on your own screen first. The clock, weather, countdowns and one calendar stay free after the trial.

Does it work with Google Calendar?

Yes. Calendars from your iPhone or iPad appear on the screen, including Google and Outlook accounts added there, plus any calendar you can subscribe to by link (including many school calendars).

When does it launch?

Very soon. Kinboard is in final testing and will be on the App Store shortly.
