Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Kinboard has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics and no ads. The developer never receives your calendars, lists, photos or location.

Where your data lives

Everything you set up in Kinboard - family members, calendars, lists, chores, countdowns, photos and settings - is stored on your devices and in your own iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit. When you invite family members, the same data is shared with the people you invite, through their iCloud accounts. The developer cannot see or access any of it.

Calendars

  • With your permission, Kinboard on iPhone or iPad reads the calendars you choose and copies their upcoming events into your iCloud so your family screen can show them. Kinboard never changes or deletes events.
  • Calendar links you add (ICS or webcal) are downloaded directly from the calendar provider by your device.

Location and weather

To show local weather, Kinboard asks for your approximate location. It is used only on your device to request a forecast from Apple Weather (WeatherKit). Kinboard does not store or share your location.

Photos

  • Family photos you add are saved to your iCloud so every family screen can show them.
  • If you choose an album from the Photos app, its pictures are read only on that device and are never uploaded.

Purchases

The free trial, the Kinboard+ purchase and the three optional tips in Tip Jar are handled by Apple through the App Store. Tips unlock nothing; they are simply a way to support the developer. Kinboard only learns whether a purchase went through and whether Kinboard+ is active; it never sees your payment details.

Diagnostics

On iPhone and iPad, iOS and iPadOS can give Kinboard reports about the app's crashes and performance (Apple MetricKit). The reports describe how the app runs and contain no calendars, lists, photos or location. Apple TV does not provide these reports.

  • The reports stay on your device: at most five of each kind, for no longer than seven days, and they are excluded from backups.
  • They reach the developer only if you turn on Attach Diagnostics to Email (off by default) and send the email from Write to the Developer yourself. It goes to support@kinboard-family.app.
  • That email also includes your device model identifier, system version, app version, app language and region, and whether Kinboard+ is active. It never includes your device name or any family data.
  • The developer uses the reports only to fix problems and deletes them when they are no longer needed.

Children

Kinboard is a family app and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Tracking

Kinboard does not track you across apps or websites and contains no third-party analytics or advertising code. This website sets no cookies and loads nothing from other servers; it only remembers your choice of light or dark appearance in your browser.

Deleting your data

Delete the app from your devices, then remove its iCloud data in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Kinboard. Family members who joined your family can leave it at any time from Kinboard on their iPhone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions about privacy: privacy@kinboard-family.app.
